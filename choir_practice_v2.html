👋 Welcome! Here's how to get started
  1. Find the score files — your conductor has uploaded the MuseScore files to the choir Google Drive. Download the .mscz file for the piece you want to practise.
  2. Load the score — drag the file into the box below, or tap it to browse.
  3. Select your voice part — tap your part (e.g. Alto, Tenor 1). You can select multiple parts to hear them together.
  4. Play and practise — press ▶ Play all, or tap any bar in the list to start from there. Use 🔁 Loop range to repeat a section.
📱 On iPhone or iPad: make sure the physical silence switch on the side of the phone is turned off (no orange showing), or plug in headphones. The sound will not play if the phone is silenced.
